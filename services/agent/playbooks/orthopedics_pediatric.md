# Pediatric orthopedics
Fractures (the largest share), scoliosis, hip dysplasia, clubfoot, limb differences and young athletes. Parents
drive and schedule around school, so after-school hours matter; Medicaid and CHIP cover a large share of Texas
children. The children's hospital (Texas Children's in Houston) is both the main referrer and the main competitor;
pediatricians and urgent cares send fractures.
Must keep:
- `child|pediatric|teen|under 18|u18|kids` Children and teens nearby
- `medicaid|chip` Children on Medicaid and CHIP
- `pediatrician|children's hospital|referr` Pediatricians and the children's hospital (referrer and competitor)
- `high school|athlet|student` High schools and youth sports
