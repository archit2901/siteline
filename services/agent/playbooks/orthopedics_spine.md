# Spine
Most spine patients never have surgery: the clinic manages back and neck pain with imaging, injections and PT, and a
small share go on to fusion or decompression. Working-age adults with heavy-labor jobs (workers' comp) and adults
45-75 with degenerative disease are the core. Referrals come from primary care, pain management, chiropractors and
PT; neurosurgery and pain groups are competitors for some patients and referrers for others.
Site: MRI access within the clinic or a nearby imaging center with short scheduling, a fluoroscopy suite for
injections, parking for patients who cannot walk far.
Must keep:
- `primary care|referr|pain|chiro` Referral base: primary care, pain management, chiropractic
- `mri|imaging` MRI access (own magnet or an imaging center nearby)
- `job|work|labor|workers` Heavy-labor jobs and workers' comp back injuries
