# Team coverage (the practice covers school, college or club teams)
A practice that staffs sidelines or supplies athletic trainers gets the injured athletes those schools produce:
the ATC triages on Friday night and books the covering physician's Saturday injury clinic or Monday slot. The
clinic's distance from those schools decides whether that pipeline holds, and the other large high schools near a
candidate site are the next schools the practice could cover. Treat a named school as a partner (its athletes
are referred), never as a competitor; a nearby orthopedic group already covering a school is a competitor for that
school's referrals.
Must keep:
- `team|school|athlet|sideline|trainer` Drive time from the site to the schools the practice covers
- `high school|uil|student|5a|6a` Other large high schools near the site (the next schools to cover)
