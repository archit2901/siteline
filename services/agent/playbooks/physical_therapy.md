# Physical therapy
- Referrals from orthopedic surgeons and sports medicine drive volume: being near them is positive.
- Patients come 2-3 times a week for weeks: short drive time and fast parking beat visibility.
- Workers' comp and commercial payers dominate; employers and industrial jobs matter.
- Competition: PT chains and hospital outpatient rehab. Space: open gym, can be upstairs with an elevator.
Must keep:
- `orthopedi|referr|surgeon` Nearby orthopedic and sports medicine referrers
- `drive|travel|parking` Short drives and fast parking for patients who come 2-3 times a week
