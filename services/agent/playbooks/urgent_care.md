# Urgent care and walk-in clinics
- Decisions are made in the moment: visibility from a busy arterial, signage, a retail center with a grocery
  anchor, and easy left-turn access matter more than for any other clinic.
- Demand: households with children and working adults, commercial insurance, daytime workers near offices.
- Competition: other urgent cares, freestanding ERs, retail clinics in pharmacies, telehealth; ERs absorb overflow.
- Hours: evenings and weekends; staff commute and safety at night.
