# Hand and upper extremity
Carpal tunnel, trigger finger, fractures, tendon lacerations and arthritis. Many procedures are done in the office
under local anesthesia (WALANT), so a procedure room matters more than OR access. Certified hand therapists (CHT) are
the post-op partner; manual and industrial jobs supply workers' comp injuries; emergency departments send tendon
and fracture follow-ups within days.
Must keep:
- `job|work|industrial|manual|workers` Manual and industrial jobs nearby (workers' comp hand injuries)
- `hand therap|occupational therap|\bcht\b` Hand therapists nearby
- `emergency|\ber\b|urgent care` Emergency departments and urgent cares that send injuries
