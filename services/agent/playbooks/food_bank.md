# Food banks and pantries
- Need: people below 185% of poverty (TEFAP), SNAP households, children and seniors in poverty, low food access
  (USDA low-income/low-access tracts), households without a car.
- Clients carry food home: bus stops at the door and short walks matter; drive-through distributions need lots.
- Gaps, not duplication: distance to existing pantries and partner agencies; churches, schools and community
  centers are hosts and referral partners.
- Operations: loading dock or truck access, cold storage, volunteers (who live or work nearby), evening hours for
  working families. Safety and dignity of the line; flood risk for stored food.
Must keep:
- `poverty|food insecur|snap|tefap` Food-insecure households (poverty, SNAP)
- `pantr|partner|existing|gap` Distance to existing pantries and partner agencies (fill gaps, don't duplicate)
- `transit|bus|car|walk` Transit and walking access for clients who carry food home
