# Occupational orthopedics (workers' comp)
Injuries happen where people work, not where they live: count jobs by industry (construction, manufacturing,
warehouse and logistics, energy, port) near the site. Employers, adjusters and case managers pick the clinic, value
fast first visits and return-to-work reports, and send injured workers by car from job sites, so freeway access and
same-day slots matter. Occupational medicine clinics and urgent cares with employer contracts are both referrers
(they send injuries they can't treat) and competitors.
Must keep:
- `job|work|industr|employ` Jobs nearby in injury-prone industries (LODES)
- `occupational|urgent care|employer` Occupational medicine clinics and employer contracts
- `freeway|highway|road access|drive` Freeway access from job sites
