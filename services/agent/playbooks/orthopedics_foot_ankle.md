# Foot and ankle
Two populations: athletes with sprains, Achilles and fractures, and older or diabetic adults with degenerative and
diabetic foot problems. Podiatrists are the main competitor and a referral source; wound care and vascular
clinics refer diabetic feet. Patients in walking boots and on knee scooters need parking at the door.
Must keep:
- `diabet|places` Diabetes burden (CDC PLACES, a need proxy)
- `podiatr` Podiatrists: competitors and referrers
- `parking|step-free|accessib|scooter|boot` Step-free entry and parking at the door
